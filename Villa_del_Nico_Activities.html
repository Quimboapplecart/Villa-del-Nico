Villa del Nico is located 9km north of Pagudpud town centre. Cool off in our swimming pools. For the adventurous, why not become acquainted with the abundant underwater sea life and many beautiful corals. The Villa provides a peaceful location for you, your friends and family to take a quite break from your busy days.

With over 60 meters of secluded private unspoilt beach frontage it’s suitable for Birthdays, Family Reunions, Team Building & Weddings. 

The Villa is “Self Catering” with fully equipped "Open Plan" Kitchen - Dinning - Living areas. However if you prefer to eat out there are restaurants nearby offering local and international cuisine.

Experience the warm blue waters of Pagudpud's breathtaking “SAUD” beach.
