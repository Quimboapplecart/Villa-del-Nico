Reservations


To confirm your reservation, an advance "Down Payment" equivalent to fifty percent (50%) of your total Villa / Room hire costs is required. Payments should be deposited into our company PNB account. (Details e-mailed once booking initiated) Please keep the Bank deposit slip / official receipt to show upon arrival.


Cancellations


Cancellations should be made not less than seven (7) days prior to your arrival, otherwise the deposited amount will be forfeited in our favor. However, if your booking is moved to a later date, the deposit can still be applied.
