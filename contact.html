Reservations

To confirm reservations, an advance down payment equivalent to 50% of the total Villa hire cost is required.

Payments may be transferred or deposited into PNB Bank, G-Cash or PayPal accounts.

Payment details are emailed once a booking is initiated.

Cancellations

Cancellations must be made at least 7 days before arrival; otherwise, the deposited amount will be forfeited. If the booking is moved to a later date, the deposit may still be applied.
