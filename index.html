Welcome to Villa del Nico — Your Private Beachfront Escape in Pagudpud

Discover Villa del Nico, a private beachfront holiday villa in Caparispisan, Pagudpud, Ilocos Norte, Philippines. Enjoy a peaceful coastal getaway with family and friends, away from crowded hotels and busy tourist areas.

Villa del Nico is rented as an entire villa, giving your group exclusive use of the accommodation and facilities. With four sleeping areas accommodating up to 25 overnight guests, it is ideal for family holidays, reunions, birthday celebrations, company team-building activities and intimate beachfront weddings.

Spend your days enjoying the swimming pools, relaxing on the beach, playing basketball or volleyball, or gathering for a barbecue. Whether you are planning a family gathering, celebrating a special occasion or organising a corporate retreat, Villa del Nico offers the privacy and flexibility of an entire beachfront property.

Located in the beautiful coastal municipality of Pagudpud, Villa del Nico is a welcoming base for exploring Ilocos Norte or enjoying a private seaside retreat.

For weddings, birthdays and corporate events involving additional daytime visitors, please contact us to discuss your requirements and arrangements.
