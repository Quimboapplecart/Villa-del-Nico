  • Approaching Pagudpud turn left off Pan Philippine Highway immediately after SHELL gasoline station
  • Drive 1 km until reaching Pagudpud Municipal Hall, turn right
  • Drive 2 km until reaching white Gazebo structure at “T” Junction, turn left
  • Drive 3.5 km across river passing Saud Beach Hotel entrance, continue until reaching the “Welcome to Barangay Caparispisan” signage board
  • Turn left crossing the river passing the Police check point & Caparispisan day Centre on your right
  • Turn left, drive further 2.5 km until reaching "Fast Food / Snack Bar"
  • Turn left, drive to end of access road
  • If the main Villa gates are closed, sound your car horn and staff will open

If your stopped entering the access road to Villa del Nico by the "Fast Food / Snack Bar" security personnel just explain that  you have a reservation / booking at Villa del Nico 

Have a safe trip, and enjoy the peace and tranquillity
