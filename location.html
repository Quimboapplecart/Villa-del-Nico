Villa del Nico · Caparispisan, Pagudpud

Welcome to Villa del Nico

Discover the quieter side of Ilocos Norte, surrounded by coastal scenery and tropical landscapes.

Use the map to navigate, then follow the local landmark directions below as a helpful guide.

Road layouts, businesses and landmarks may change. Please confirm directions before travelling.

Driving Directions to Villa del Nico

From the Pan-Philippine Highway approaching Pagudpud:

01

Leave the highway

Approaching Pagudpud from the South or North, turn off the Pan-Philippine Highway at the white shell sculpture and head to the centre of Pagudpud.

02

Pagudpud Municipal Hall

When reaching Pagudpud Municipal Hall, turn right.

03

Cabacanan River to Capraspisan River Bridge

Drive a further 3.5 km, crossing the large Cabacanan River. Continue past Saud Beach Hotel until you reach the Capraspisan River bridge on your left.

04

Cross the small river

Cross the small river, turn left when reaching the police checkpoint and Caparispisan Day Centre on your right.

05

Villa access road

Drive 2.5 km, turn left onto the Villa access road when reaching “Breakfast at IMA cafe”.

06

Arrive at Villa del Nico

If the main gates are closed, sound your car horn and staff will open. If questioned at the access road, explain that you have a Villa del Nico reservation.

Before You Arrive — Groceries & Cooking Supplies

If you’re planning to cook meals during your stay, we recommend buying your groceries (meat, vegetables, fruit, dairy, cooking oil & seasonings) before arriving at the Villa, as your nearest large marketplace is 9 km away, back at the Pagudpud white shell statue.

Local Visitor Information

For current Pagudpud visitor information and contact details, please refer to the official Pagudpud website.
